# The human's part: walk them through it (§5)

Some steps are genuinely theirs: legal acceptances (SDK licences, terms), credentials and logins,
payments and account enrolment, `sudo`, anything on production, and judgement calls. For each:

- Say **why** it is theirs, in one clause.
- Give the **exact commands**, copy-pasteable, for *their* OS and shell. Include how to load any env
  file you created.
- Say **what they will see** (prompts, windows, a download size, how long it takes) and what to tell
  you when it's done.
- Do everything around it yourself. Don't hand them five steps when one needs them.

Never ask them to paste a secret, token or password into the chat.
